# FitCheck Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

FitCheck is an iOS app that keeps a digital wardrobe, builds outfits from it, and uses AI to rate outfits and show how they would look on you. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and what you can do about it.

If you have a question about this policy, write to [bakhrom86157@icloud.com](mailto:bakhrom86157@icloud.com).

## The short version

- FitCheck has no accounts. You do not sign up, and we do not ask for your email address or phone number.
- Your wardrobe, outfits, profile and photos are stored on your device.
- Photos leave your device only when you use an AI feature that needs to look at them.
- The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.
- We do not sell your information.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your iPhone and nowhere else:

- **Profile details you enter:** your name, the cut of clothes you wear, style preferences, and, if you choose to add them, your height, body shape, clothing and shoe sizes, budget and lifestyle.
- **Photos:** pictures of your clothes, an optional selfie used for colour analysis, an optional full-length photo used for AI try-on, and the images the app generates for you.
- **Wardrobe and outfit data:** the items you add, the outfits you save or log as worn, your streak and usage statistics.
- **App settings:** language, reminder time, shop region, notification preference, token balance and subscription status.

This information stays on your device unless one of the features described below sends part of it out. If you use iCloud or computer backups, Apple may include the app's data in those backups under Apple's own terms.

## Information that leaves your device

### AI features

When you use an AI feature, the app sends the data that feature needs to our server, which passes it on to our AI processing provider, kie.ai. The features are outfit rating (Fit Check), clothing recognition when you add an item, colour analysis of your selfie, AI outfit suggestions, and AI try-on.

Depending on the feature, the data sent may include:

- the photo you chose for that feature, such as an outfit photo, a garment photo, your selfie or your full-length photo;
- photos of the garments in the outfit being tried on;
- a text description of your wardrobe items and of the preferences you entered: the cut you wear, body shape, height, lifestyle and the colours that suit you;
- the occasion, style and weather the outfit is for.

Your name is not included in these requests.

For the AI model to read a photo, the photo is uploaded to a temporary file host operated for the AI provider. Files uploaded there are deleted automatically after 24 hours. During that time a file is reachable through a long, hard-to-guess web address that is not published anywhere.

Photos of your face and body are personal, and you decide whether to share them. The selfie and the full-length photo are optional, and the app works without them.

The AI provider processes this data on our behalf to return a result. Its own handling of data is described in its privacy policy at [kie.ai](https://kie.ai).

### Our server

AI requests go through a server we run, so that the key to the AI service is never stored in the app. The server does not keep your photos, your wardrobe or the answers it passes back. It keeps:

- a random token that identifies your installation of the app, created the first time an AI feature is used. It is not tied to your name, email address or Apple ID;
- the internet address the installation was registered from, to limit abuse;
- a daily count of how many AI requests the installation has made, to enforce usage limits;
- the web addresses of the photos uploaded for AI processing, for up to three days, so that only the installation that uploaded a photo can use it.

Server request logs, which include internet addresses, are kept for a short time for troubleshooting.

### Weather

To match outfits to the weather, the app looks up your approximate location once a day from your internet address, using the service [geojs.io](https://www.geojs.io). It then requests a forecast for that approximate location from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com). The app does not ask for location permission and does not use GPS. Only the city name and the forecast are kept on your device.

### Shopping suggestions

When you open "where to buy" for a missing piece, the app sends a search query, such as "white sneakers", directly from your device to the online stores it searches. Those stores receive the query and, as with any web request, your internet address. Tapping a product or a store link opens that store's website, which is governed by the store's own privacy policy.

### Purchases

Subscriptions and token packs are bought through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your card number or billing details. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device as local notifications. They are not sent from a server, and turning them on does not share anything with us.

### Home screen widget

If you add the FitCheck widget, the app shares today's outfit with the widget on the same device. Nothing leaves the device for this.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** to take photos of your clothes and of yourself.
- **Photo library:** to choose existing photos and to save your looks to Photos.
- **Notifications:** to show outfit and streak reminders.

Each permission is requested when you first use the feature that needs it. You can change any of them later in iOS Settings.

## How we use information

Information is used only to provide the app's features: building and rating outfits, generating try-on images, matching outfits to the weather, finding where to buy missing pieces, and sending the reminders you turned on. We do not use it for advertising and do not build profiles of you for third parties.

## Sharing

We share information only with the service providers named above, and only as far as each feature requires. We may also disclose information if the law requires it. We do not sell personal information.

## Keeping and deleting your data

- Data on your device stays there until you delete it. You can remove individual items and photos in the app.
- **Profile → Reset app** erases your profile, wardrobe, outfits and stored photos from the device.
- Deleting the app removes all data the app stored on the device.
- Photos uploaded for AI processing are deleted from the temporary file host after 24 hours.

Because the app has no accounts, we hold no copy of your wardrobe or profile on our own servers and cannot look up or restore your data. What our server does keep is described under "Our server" above.

## Children

FitCheck is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the AI features with their photos, contact us at the address below.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Most of this you can do directly in the app, since the data is on your device. For anything else, write to us and we will respond within 30 days.

## Security

Data sent to the services above travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## International transfers

The service providers named above may process data in countries other than the one you live in.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last revised. Significant changes will be noted in the app or in the release notes.
